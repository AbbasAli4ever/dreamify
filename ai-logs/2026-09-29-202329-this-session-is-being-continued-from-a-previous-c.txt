  How it works
  - Matching: when a dream is analysed, Gemini also writes a one-line anonymous
    overview of it, e.g. "Walking through warm rain on an empty street at night,
    calm but a little lonely." The database then finds other people's dreams
    within ±10 days using three signals:
    - vibe: how close the overviews are in meaning (I tested this live with
      Gemini: similar dreams score clearly higher than unrelated ones);
    - shared symbols, like rain;
    - shared feelings.
  - Each match gets a % alike score.
  - Privacy: other people see only your first name, your photo and that one
    line, never your dream, title or artwork. A switch in Settings turns it off
    both ways: you're hidden and you see no one.

  What users see
  - Dream page: a new section with lines like "3 dreamers saw something like
    this within 10 days of you. Maya dreamt of rain 4 days before you."
    - It has a graph with your dream in the middle and dreamers' avatars around
      it, closer the more alike.
    - Tapping an avatar opens a popup: name, when they dreamt, % alike, their
      dream in one line, "You both dreamt of" and "You both felt".
    - It also shows how common each symbol was that week, e.g. "12% of dreamers
      dreamt of rain around this night".
  - /kindred ("your dream circle"): everyone from the last 60 days in one graph,
    plus counts (dreamers, dreams shared, nights in a row) and a list with the
    closest first.
  - Home: a card with overlapping avatars, "8 dreamers dreamt like you lately"
    and your dream streak (nights in a row with a dream).

  Your older dreams are added automatically the first time the app opens. The
